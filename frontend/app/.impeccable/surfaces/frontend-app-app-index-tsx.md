Audience: friend groups and gamers installing the app (Android first, the owner's phone).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Your profile in your hand: one example profile card you tilt and swipe, and every swipe re-lights the whole screen in the next theme. Refuses the onboarding carousel of illustrations and dots.

OWN-WORLD: The Lit Room on a phone: ink ground, the three-light field of the current theme, Funnel Display 800 for the one title, Atkinson for reading, pills, the holo card with foil only on its edge, the theme's action colour on the one pill.
