# Landing page (/)

Scope: public landing page. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: friend groups and gamers on a first visit, evenings, often a second monitor next to a game or a phone in a dim room.
Action: understand "one app instead of four" and open Realm in the browser (open early-access sign-up). Secondary: find the download options (all native apps coming soon).
Proof/content: real mini-UI previews of each region built from real components, with illustrative demo content labelled as such. Honest availability markers (available now vs coming soon). No metrics, testimonials, user counts, or partner logos exist, so none appear.
Constraints: DMs are not end-to-end encrypted during early access and the page says so; E2EE claims only for what ships before public launch. Store buttons are Realm's own "coming soon" buttons until listings exist. No em-dashes, no eyebrow labels, one label per CTA intent.
Memorable moment: the magenta course drawing itself across the night map and ending where all four regions meet.
Unresolved: final brand mark (wordmark only for now); real screenshots replace illustrative previews once the app ships.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Realm is one continent; Spaces, Feed, Profiles and Boards are regions of the same map that all meet at one hub. It refuses the category default of a screenshot-in-a-browser-frame hero over a feature-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Modern cartography, never fantasy parchment. Flat map inks on a deep blue-green night ground; the day edition is cool map-paper white. Region inks: green Spaces, yellow Feed, blue Profiles, earth-brown Boards. Magenta orienteering overprint is the only accent and only ever marks an action (course, controls, primary button). Archivo superfamily: expanded caps for region lettering, condensed for labels, tabular figures for grid references. Thin ruled legend panels, 4px corners, contour-line texture, a functional A-D by 1-4 coordinate grid.

STORY: The visitor sees four familiar kinds of app drawn as one landmass, learns that each region is a real part of Realm with honest now/soon markers, and opens Realm in the browser; downloads wait in the legend.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left five columns: H1 "Four apps. One Realm.", a subline of 20 words or fewer, a magenta "Open Realm" button and an outline "Download" button. Right seven columns bleeding to the edge: the night map with grid ticks, four inked regions meeting at the hub, the magenta course from a start triangle through region controls to a finish double-circle at the hub, and a legend panel bottom-right holding the key and the editions (browser open now, desktop and mobile coming soon). Signature interaction: hovering or focusing a region isolates it (others dim, its contours tighten) and unfolds a real mini UI anchored to it.

FORM: Candidate 7 of 7 (game-box fold-out world map, printed as a modern topographic sheet); seed key d5630a04. Raises: night and day editions; one action accent; functional grid; isolate-and-dim; contour density as state.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
