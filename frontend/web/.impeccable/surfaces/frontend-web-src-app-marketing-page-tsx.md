# Landing page (/)

Scope: public landing page. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: friend groups and gamers on a first visit, evenings, often a second monitor next to a game or a phone in a dim room.
Action: understand "one app instead of four" and open Realm in the browser (open early-access sign-up). Secondary: find the download options (all native apps coming soon).
Proof/content: real mini-UI previews of each region built from real components, with illustrative demo content labelled as such. Honest availability markers (available now vs coming soon). No metrics, testimonials, user counts, or partner logos exist, so none appear.
Constraints: DMs are not end-to-end encrypted during early access and the page says so; E2EE claims only for what ships before public launch. Store buttons are Realm's own "coming soon" buttons until listings exist. No em-dashes, no eyebrow labels, one label per CTA intent.
Memorable moment: stepping into another universe: the live 3D eclipse with the astronaut crew waving from orbit, then the lime flight path drawing itself across the star chart and landing at the eclipse.
Brand: the Eclipse mark, Aurora palette and astronaut explorers were picked by the user from the concept sheet on 2026-09-27 (brand/concepts.png); they replace the earlier user-supplied gateway logo, which the user rejected. Unresolved: real screenshots replace illustrative previews once the app ships.

## Direction contract

Revised 2026-09-27 after the user rejected the continent world ("I do want a spacy look, going in another universe", "not fully cartoonish", "a more modern color look", "the spaces look too much like Discord") and picked Eclipse / Aurora / Astronaut explorers from the concept sheet. Earlier asks still bind: keep all the information, add lots of motion, 3D models and images, readable for dyslexic and short-attention visitors, discord.com as the quality bar.

THESIS: Realm is another universe your group steps into together: one eclipse at the centre, four worlds (Spaces, Feed, Profiles, Boards) in orbit around it. It refuses both the screenshot-in-a-browser-frame hero over a feature-card grid and the chat-app look (server-icon rail plus channel list).

OWN-WORLD: Midnight space (#060d16 ground) lit by an aurora: lime (#b1ef4a) is the one action colour and the eclipse's rim light; teal, ice blue, violet and pink are the four worlds. Semi-cartoon 3D in glossy clearcoat plastic with iridescent visors (astronaut explorers, props, planets), rendered live in the hero and as transparent WebP renders elsewhere. Glassy speech bubbles with aurora edges. Unbounded for display lettering, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for everything you read. Orbit rings and star-chart locators as the recurring motif. Motion everywhere, all of it pausable.

STORY: The visitor arrives in the universe (hero), hears the chatter of a busy Realm (sticker marquee), sees the whole app at once (showcase), learns the map (star chart with fold-out previews), visits each world with honest now/soon markers, reads how privacy really works, finds downloads, and is waved through by the crew (closing).

FIRST VIEWPORT: left, the headline "One place to [chat / post / show off / vote / hang out] with your crew." with the rotating word in aurora colours, a short subline, "Open Realm" and "Download". Right, the live 3D universe: the eclipse with its aurora corona, four planets on tilted orbits with name tags, four astronauts floating in zero-g (one waving), speech bubbles pinned to them, floating props, a rendered poster while it loads, edges fading into the page.

FORM: user-chosen from the concept sheet (logo 2 "Eclipse", palette "Aurora", characters "Astronaut explorers"); no concept roll was run for this world, so there is no seed key.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance (frontend/web/brand/README.md).
