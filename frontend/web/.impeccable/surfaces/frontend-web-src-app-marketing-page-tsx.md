# Landing page (/)

Scope: public landing page. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: friend groups and gamers on a first visit, evenings, often a second monitor next to a game or a phone in a dim room.
Action: understand "one app instead of four" and open Realm in the browser (open early-access sign-up). Secondary: find the download options (all native apps coming soon).
Proof/content: real mini-UI previews of each region built from real components, with illustrative demo content labelled as such. Honest availability markers (available now vs coming soon). No metrics, testimonials, user counts, or partner logos exist, so none appear.
Constraints: DMs are not end-to-end encrypted during early access and the page says so; E2EE claims only for what ships before public launch. Store buttons are Realm's own "coming soon" buttons until listings exist. No em-dashes, no eyebrow labels, one label per CTA intent.
Memorable moment: the lit course (the logo's path) drawing itself across the night map and ending where all four regions meet.
Brand: user-supplied logo is binding (PRODUCT.md). Unresolved: real screenshots replace illustrative previews once the app ships.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Realm is one continent; Spaces, Feed, Profiles and Boards are regions of the same map that all meet at one hub. It refuses the category default of a screenshot-in-a-browser-frame hero over a feature-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: A night map keyed to the Realm logo (a lit gateway and a winding path into a night landscape). Deep indigo ground from the logo's sky; flat region inks in mint (Spaces), amber (Feed), sky (Profiles) and coral (Boards); one violet-magenta overprint taken from the logo's path that only ever marks an action (course, controls, primary button). The course is the logo's path made into a map route, and it alone carries the logo's light (a soft glow), once, in the hero. Archivo superfamily: expanded caps for map lettering, condensed for labels, tabular figures for grid references. Thin ruled legend panels, 4px corners, contour-line texture, a functional A-D by 1-4 coordinate grid. The logo tile is the brand mark in the header.

STORY: The visitor sees four familiar kinds of app drawn as one landmass, learns that each region is a real part of Realm with honest now/soon markers, and opens Realm in the browser; downloads wait in the legend.

FIRST VIEWPORT (raised 2026-09-27 at the user's request for Discord-level richness): full-bleed night scene drawn from the logo. Centered extended-caps headline "Four apps. One Realm." in one line, a subline of 20 words or fewer, "Open Realm" and "Download". Below it the lit gateway stands on the horizon with the real Realm app window inside it, the path of light running from the viewer to the gate, a phone showing the mobile feed, and floating live fragments (mention, friends online, reactions, a board thread). The Atlas map follows immediately as the second act ("Everything has a place"), keeping hover-to-isolate and the drawn course. Region sections are luminous stages lit in their ink. The closing bookend shows the whole gateway with "Open Realm" inside the arch.

FORM: Candidate 7 of 7 (game-box fold-out world map, printed as a modern topographic sheet); seed key d5630a04. Raises: night and day editions; one action accent; functional grid; isolate-and-dim; contour density as state.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
