# Design System: Realm

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Lit Room"**

Realm's world is a dark room lit by the colour of whoever's profile is in view. The ground is a near-black ink (the ink ground), and behind everything three soft colour fields glow, taken from the active profile theme (Mint by default; Orchid, Aqua and Ember when the theme swaps) under a fine grain. On the front page they drift as you scroll; everywhere else they hold still. The page does not decorate itself: the real app does the showing, while big tight Funnel Display headlines carry the story and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next carries every word you read.

The world has one moving idea: your colour takes over the page. The action colour, the light, the mark's inner dot, the focus ring, the selection and the 3D rim light all read from the same theme variables, and a theme change cross-fades all of them together (0.8s). On the front page those variables sit on the world root (`[data-world="canvas"]`) and follow the profile in view; on every other screen they are the person's own theme, set on `<html>` (`data-accent`), so sign-in and the app wear the colour you picked. Night is the default and only edition of this world for now; a day edition and user-made themes come later, under the paid "make it yours" theming plan in `docs/THEMING.md` (the plan lives there, not here).

It rejects the previous world (the space diorama, molten amber, aurora and nebula, astronauts and mascots floating loose as stickers), the template dark SaaS page of feature cards on black, and, inside the app, both a Discord-style server rail and the generic sidebar-plus-cards dashboard. Density is low and cinematic on the front page (one statement per viewport, the product beside it) and in the app it should feel like a real app: a labelled nav column on the lit ground beside one solid sheet, what's live with your people up front, hairlines inside, and little to press besides joining what's live. In the app the Instagram and Threads feel (faces, posters, a wall of what your people are doing) leads a little over the Discord feel (rooms, voice, who's in), which stays present; Realm clones neither (the owner, 2026-10-05).

**Key Characteristics:**
- Ink ground lit by three theme-coloured fields plus grain; never flat black, never a starfield. Moving on the front page, still everywhere else.
- One action colour per screen, and it is the person's own theme.
- The real app UI, framed in black-bezel phones and rounded window frames, is the imagery.
- Funnel Display set tight and huge for statements and names; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading.
- Pills everywhere you act or read a status; generous rounded rectangles for frames and panels.
- In the app: a labelled nav column on the lit ground beside one solid Surface sheet; content straight on the sheet under hairlines, never boxes in boxes.
- Native scroll; scrubbed sticky stages; a full static fallback for anyone who isn't moving.

A near-black neutral ground with cool grey inks, lit by one of four swappable theme palettes; colour comes from light, not from painted surfaces.

### Themes
Four themes, each writing only six values: the accent, its "on" colour, three lights and a `glow`. Mint is the default on `:root`; Orchid, Aqua and Ember apply through `:root[data-accent="…"]`. The glow is solved per theme so the light field's brightest point stays at relative luminance 0.075: Mint 0.6, Orchid 0.78, Aqua 0.55, Ember 0.6. A pick is remembered on the device (`realm:accent`, applied by the boot script before first paint) and saved to the account.

### Primary
- **Theme Action** (Mint Action by default; Orchid, Aqua, Ember Action when swapped): the one action colour. See the One Action Rule for every place it may appear. Text on it is always its paired "on" colour (On Mint, On Orchid, On Aqua, On Ember); every pair passes 4.5:1, and every accent passes 4.5:1 as text on every surface and on the field's brightest point.

### Secondary
- **Theme Light Fields** (Light 1, 2, 3 per theme): the three colours the light field blends behind the page, and the profile banner, clip art, the holo card frame and the colour swatches (a 135° gradient through the three). Never used for text or for UI fills you act on. Live in CSS as `--r-light-1..3` (and `--l1`–`--l3` on the front page).

### Neutral
- **Ink Ground**: the page itself, the light field's base, the hero-foot fade, the header's 72% glass, field fills (70%), the scrims and the dark chips (60–75%).
- **Ink**: headlines, primary text and text links.
- **Ink 2**: leads, body copy, nav links, row descriptions, tag text, hints.
- **Ink 3**: the quietest text and placeholders. Kept almost as light as Ink 2 on purpose; see the Worst Point Rule.
- **Surface**: the app's content sheet, the auth card and the menu panel.
- **Raised**: popovers, the Travel dialog, Here now while it lies over the page, hover and open fills on the bar's icon buttons and quiet controls.
- **Frame Box** (`surface-sunken`): inside a surface: the verify banner, boxes, other people's chat bubbles (`bubble-theirs`), voice tiles, and the ground under a photo or map while it loads.
- **Rule** (`line`): hairlines and dividers, decorative only.
- **Line Strong**: edges that identify a control (fields, the ghost pill, the New Space poster, the Invite face's dashed circle) and the ring of a face that's away or busy: 3:1 against the ground and every surface.
- **Frame Surface / Bezel**: the front page's app window frame and phone ring.
- **Hairline** (white at 6–12%): inset rings on panels, tags, chips, popovers and dialogs.
- **Presence**: "someone is here right now": the small dot; a face's 2.5px ring and the speaker badge on a face or a Space badge (LiveBadge) while someone's in voice; the "N here now" toggle in a Space's header; the live room on a poster and the live line on a Live tile; a voice room's head count in its tab (15% fill, Presence text); the Live strip's wash (10% fading to 2%) and 24% ring; and the call dock's speaker. Nothing else.
- **Danger / Danger Line**: error text and icons; the edge of a field in error and the notice ring; the text and 1.5px outline of a destructive action (see the Outline Danger Rule); the muted-mic slash and the muted mic toggle's outline. Always with words and an icon, never colour alone.
- **Success**: the "done" notice and the email-confirmed line.

### Named Rules
**The Worst Point Rule.** Every grey text tone must pass 4.5:1 against the light field's brightest possible point, held at relative luminance 0.075. That is why Ink 2 and Ink 3 are as light as they are, and why each theme carries a `glow` factor solved so its brightest point stays at 0.075. `tokens.test.ts` checks it for every theme. A new theme ships with its glow solved, or it doesn't ship.

**The One Action Rule.** The theme colour is the person's, and it appears only on: the one action per screen (the one action pill), focus (rings and the field's focus edge), "needs you" (the "New" chip on a Space poster and the "New" line in chat: a word, never a number), the Me avatar, and your own chat bubbles (decided by the owner on 2026-10-04, and shipped: `bubble-mine` and `on-bubble-mine` follow the accent). Something live you can drop into carries Join as an action pill (the owner, 2026-10-05): each Live tile on Home and the Live strip in a room. In a room, the one action is the composer's round Send; on a voice room before you join, Join voice; in a sheet, its submit (Add this place, Send report). Everything else that acts is a ghost or quiet pill or a text link. Product previews (the sign-up profile card, the front page's frames) show it as the product wears it; they are inert art, never controls. Messages (built 2026-10) puts the accent on four things this list does not name, and the owner has not ruled on them: the count of waiting conversations on Messages in the nav column and the phone tab (a number, where this rule says a word), the unread dot on an inbox row (a Space row's unread dot is Ink), your own reaction chips (a 1.5px accent ring on a 15% accent fill), and the picked check circle in New message (a selection, which elsewhere is never the accent). Until they are ruled on, don't copy them to other surfaces.

**The Light Is The Colour Rule.** Hue enters the page as light (the field, the banner, the 3D rim light, the swatches), never as tinted panels or coloured box-shadow glows on UI.

**The Re-light Rule.** A colour change is never a snap. The accent, its "on" colour, the three lights and the glow are registered with `@property` and cross-fade over 0.8s (the in-out curve outside the front page, `ease` on it), and stop for reduced motion and Pause motion.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from light and frames, not from a shadow scale. The light field sits behind everything; frames and panels are near-black objects on it, separated by a 1px inset hairline ring and one long, soft, black drop shadow that sinks them into the dark. Things that open over the page step up a tone (Raised), take a firmer ring and a shorter, darker shadow, and dim the page under them. The 3D phones are real objects lit by a studio of soft light panels plus a theme-coloured rim light behind them.

### Named Rules
**The Black Shadows Rule.** Shadows are black, long and soft, offset straight down. No coloured glow shadows, no hard offsets. The one coloured ring is focus: a field's 4px accent halo at 22%, spread only, no blur.

**The Lifted Overlay Rule.** A panel that opens over the page always gets all three: the scrim, the Raised fill with the 12% ring, and the overlay shadow, so it reads as lifted over the page, not as the page cut short.

**The One Blur Rule.** At most one small backdrop blur per screen, and the header owns it: the front page's ink glass (72% ink ground, blur-xl, saturate 150%). ## Components

### Buttons (pills)
Plain, bold, round. One helper (`components/pill.ts`) makes every pill in the account and app screens: `pill(kind, size)`.
- **Shape:** full pill (999px). Rounded rectangles are for content, never for buttons.
- **Action:** theme action fill, its "on" colour text, bold. The one thing to press on a screen: Sign in, Next, Create account, Home's next step.
- **Ghost:** Ink text with a 1.5px inset ring (Line Strong in the app; 22% white on the front page), Raised fill on hover. Secondary actions you'd still look for ("Enter code" in the verify banner).
- **Quiet:** Ink 2 text, no edge; Raised fill and Ink on hover. The lowest-stakes option ("Later", resend while waiting).

### FormNotice
The form's live message node is always present (`role="alert"` for errors, `status` otherwise) but takes no room while empty (`empty:absolute`), so a form doesn't jump when a message arrives or clears.

### Cards / Containers (frames)
- **Phone frame:** black body, 0.5rem bezel, Bezel ring, Phone drop shadow; the screen inside is a real app screenshot.
- **App window frame:** Frame Surface, 1.8rem, 0.6rem padding, Frame drop; boxes inside are Frame Box, 1.2rem, 0.9rem padding, inset 6% ring.
- **Border:** hairline inset rings, never solid strokes (fields and pills' Line Strong edges are the control exception).

### Signature: the light field
On the front page: a 192x108 canvas of three additive radial fields in the theme's light colours, stretched over the screen, plus an SVG-noise grain at 55% opacity in overlay blend. Fields drift with scroll position; a theme change blends over 900ms with a cubic ease-out. Redrawn only when scroll or theme moves it; still under reduced motion and Pause motion. On every other page: the same three fields drawn still in CSS behind the body (`body::before`, their strengths scaled by the theme's glow, and at 38% opacity inside the app) with the same grain (`body::after`); no canvas, no 3D.

### Signature: the turning phone tour
A React Three Fiber phone on a sticky stage, rendered only while on screen (`frameloop` "never" off screen, "demand" when still). Each step turns it half a turn through its edge, so its back never shows. Moments (dark pills, a Presence dot for live ones) float beside the phone, positioned from where the 3D stage says the phone stands, never over its screen. A 3px rail on the right fills with the action colour as the steps pass. Reduced motion, Pause motion and no WebGL get a plain static sequence: each step's copy beside a still phone frame.

### Signature: the profile canvas
An app window frame showing a profile (banner in the theme's three lights, picture, clip, "right now", Spaces, badges, and the holo card). Scrolling the pinned stage, or pressing a swatch, swaps the theme, and the whole page's light and action colour follow. The pressed swatch lifts 3px with a 2px ink-ground gap and a 2px Ink ring (0.25s, cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)). Holo foil (colour-dodge, 35%) lives only along the profile card's edge, masked out of its face.

### Signature: the loading page
Once per visit, with scripts and motion only: a phone outline draws itself in the dark as real work finishes (fonts, screens, the 3D first frame), then grows onto the hero's centre phone while the dark lifts. At least 1s, at most 3s; any input ends it; a CSS failsafe hides it at 6s.

### Motion
Native scroll, no smooth-scroll library. GSAP ScrollTrigger scrubs the sticky stages and the closing phone fan (scrub 0.6). Blocks marked to reveal rise 36px and fade in once (0.9s, power3.out, 0.08s stagger) from an already visible default. Theme variables are registered with `@property` so they cross-fade (0.8s). In the account and app screens, UI motion is an exponential ease-out (cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)) and never over 300ms: 150ms for hover and press, 200ms for swatches. Everything stops for reduced motion and the Pause motion switch.

### Named Rules
**The Outside The Bubble Rule.** Photos and videos stand on their own beside the words, at their own ratio, with the Media corner; they never sit on a coloured mat inside a bubble. Files, voice notes and places are the bubble's own content, with no card inside it.

**The Outline Danger Rule.** A destructive action is a ghost pill with Danger text and a 1.5px Danger Line ring, or a Danger row in a menu. Danger never fills a button.

**The Honest Control Rule.** Never offer a control that can't do anything. When a service isn't configured, say so plainly ("Voice calls aren't set up on this Realm yet.", "Push notifications aren't set up on this Realm yet.", "Maps aren't set up on this Realm yet.") and hide the control, or label it Coming soon.

**The Short Line Rule.** A live region speaks one short status line: the newest message from someone else, "Recording started", the pick, the result count. Never a whole list, never history as it loads.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** read colours through the role names (`bg-surface`, `text-fg-soft`, `bg-accent`, `--r-*`) outside the front page, and through `--acc`, `--on-acc`, `--l1`–`--l3` on the front page's world root, so a theme change moves the whole screen at once.
- **Do** check any new grey against luminance 0.075 at 4.5:1, and solve a `glow` for any new theme.
- **Do** keep the theme colour to the one action per screen, Join on live tiles and the Live strip, focus, "needs you" ("New"), the Me avatar and your own chat bubbles.
- **Do** put tags and chips on dark pills (60% ink ground with a hairline ring).
- **Do** show the real app inside phone or window frames, or as a still sketch, instead of illustrating it.
- **Do** say status after a step's text, with a dot, or as a tag at the row's end: "First in early access" in the action colour, "Coming soon" in Ink 2.
- **Do** label everything not built yet "Coming soon" plainly (SoonTag, Create's list), and say what will go there.
- **Do** say plainly that direct messages are not end-to-end encrypted yet, wherever DMs are offered or described, and call a community a "Space".
- **Do** put content straight on the sheet, divided by hairlines: empty states, settings sections, Home's shelves.
- **Do** give anything that opens over the page the scrim, the Raised fill, the 12% ring and the overlay shadow, and close it on Escape and a click outside, with focus moved in and back.
- **Do** list Spaces by name in the nav column, with their badge, a speaker while someone's in voice and a dot when something's new.
- **Do** keep navigation labelled at every width: the nav column's rows always carry their names, and the phone tabs their labels.
- **Do** hold the app's content in one Surface sheet beside the nav column, with the nav and the lit ground around it.
- **Do** set display type only for greetings, page titles and Space or room names, bold, at product sizes (1.2–2rem).
- **Do** show only real live things on Home: real faces, rooms with people in them right now, your actual Spaces; unread is the word "New".
- **Do** let the Instagram and Threads feel (faces, posters, live tiles) lead a little over the Discord feel, and keep Discord's live parts (voice rooms, who's in, Join) present.
- **Do** ship a plain static sequence for reduced motion, Pause motion and no WebGL, decided in CSS before first paint.
- **Do** keep the 3D phone rendering only while on screen, turning through its edge, with moments beside it.
- **Do** let photos and videos stand outside the bubble at their own ratio, and keep files, voice notes and places inside it as plain content.
- **Do** make every destructive action an outline: a ghost pill with Danger text and a 1.5px Danger Line ring, or a Danger menu row in its own group.
- **Do** say plainly when something isn't set up ("Voice calls aren't set up on this Realm yet.") or label it "Coming soon", and hide the control that can't work.
- **Do** send a person's position in a request body, for one search, rounded, and say so where it's asked for.
- **Do** keep a live region to one short status line, and keep empty live regions in place without taking room (`empty:absolute`).
- **Do** put the room you're in into view in the room tabs, on their own row under the Space's name.
- **Do** show the current tab, room, sort or reason as a neutral raised pill or an Ink ring, never in the accent.
- **Do** set Messages as the inbox beside the conversation from 64rem (the inbox 22rem, 23.5rem from 80rem, a hairline on its right), and let them take turns below that.
- **Do** label every DM and group chat with the quiet "Not end-to-end encrypted yet" chip (a dark pill in Ink 2) that opens the explainer, and say it again in the Conversation start.
- **Do** offer the quick six reactions (❤️ 😂 👍 😮 😢 🔥) first and any emoji behind the +, and let a double-click heart someone else's message.
- **Do** say "Seen" as text under your newest message, and only between people who both share read receipts.
- **Do** put the peek ("Kenji is here") in the status line above the composer, giving way to reconnecting and typing.
- **Do** leave the name off runs in a one-to-one DM; in a group chat, put the author's avatar beside the last bubble of their run.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use a smooth-scroll library or hijack the wheel.
- **Don't** add a second backdrop blur to a screen; the header owns the only one.
- **Don't** put coloured glow shadows on buttons, cards, panels or frames.
- **Don't** stamp a status pill or eyebrow above a heading.
- **Don't** nest boxes inside the sheet: no card around an empty state, a settings section or a list.
- **Don't** collapse navigation to bare icons, at any width.
- **Don't** break the app into floating boxed panels: one content sheet, not a sidebar box, a centre box and a side box.
- **Don't** use display type for labels, shelf heads or reading text, or at hero sizes inside the app.
- **Don't** build a Discord-style icon rail of Spaces; Spaces are listed by name.
- **Don't** show the current destination, a selected row or a tab in the accent; current is a neutral fill.
- **Don't** pretend a feature works: no fake counts, no fake people, no dates we can't keep. On Home nothing is padded out, and unread is never a number.
- **Don't** clone Instagram, Threads or Discord: take the feel, keep Realm's own frame, type, colour and words.
- **Don't** let a moment or overlay cover the phone's screen, or show the phone's back.
- **Don't** use translucent white fills for tags or chips.
- **Don't** mark a conversation's encryption with a lock, a shield, a warning colour or the word "encrypted" on its own while it isn't end-to-end encrypted.
- **Don't** show read receipts as ticks or faces, or to someone who doesn't share their own.
- **Don't** float the peek, or any presence, over the messages.
- **Don't** put a photo or video on a coloured mat, or a card inside a bubble.
- **Don't** fill a button with Danger or Danger Line.
- **Don't** ship a dead control: no switch, button or menu item for a service that isn't configured.
- **Don't** put anyone's location in a URL, query string or route.
- **Don't** announce whole lists, history or every keystroke through a live region.
- **Don't** put holo foil anywhere but the profile card's edge, or float mascots or stickers loose on the page.
- **Don't** bring back the old world's sky, stars, aurora, amber or Bricolage Grotesque, or use the legacy alias names, in any screen of this world.
- **Don't** use a text size or corner outside the scales above.
